Welcome to HealthEcho

HealthEcho helps you understand symptoms using trusted public-health references (WHO, ICMR, CDC, and similar), upload reports for a structured lab dashboard, and find nearby care in Amravati. It is an educational tool — not a substitute for a licensed clinician.

What you can do
💬

Guided symptom chat

The assistant asks follow-up questions when your message is too vague or unrelated, so you get safer, more relevant information.

📋

Report dashboard

Sign in to upload labs, then explore metrics, trends, a plain-language report summary, and talking points for your doctor.

🏥

Find doctors — Amravati

Browse hospitals and specialties in Amravati and book a demo appointment slot (for your own planning — confirm with the facility).

📜

History (signed-in)

Consultation history is saved only for logged-in accounts. Guests can use AI consult and Find Doctors; sign in for history, uploads, and synced reports.

🌙

Light & dark

Use the sun/moon control in the top bar to match your environment. Your choice is remembered on this device.

🔒

Privacy note

With Ollama running locally, prompts can stay on your machine. Otherwise a fallback model may be used — avoid sharing names or IDs in chat.

⚕️ HealthEcho does not diagnose or prescribe. For emergencies, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest ER immediately.
